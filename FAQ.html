Account & Billing
How do I reset my password?
Click on 'Forgot Password' at login, then follow the instructions sent to your registered email address.
What payment methods are accepted?
We accept credit/debit cards, bank transfers, and some digital wallets. See the payment page for all options.
Can I get a refund?
You may request a refund within 7 days of purchase. Refunds depend on course usage and terms; see our refund policy for full details.
Courses
How do I choose the right course for my level?
Each course page indicates a recommended level (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced). If in doubt, start with beginner courses.
Do I get a certificate after completing a course?
Yes, most courses provide a digital certificate upon meeting completion requirements (projects or exams).
Can I access course materials after finishing?
You will retain access to course materials even after completion, except for live sessions or certain limited-time offers.
Do you provide practical exercises and projects?
Yes, each technical course includes assignments or real projects to help you build your skills and portfolio.
Events
What type of events are organized?
We organize workshops, live Q&A sessions, online conferences, and short hackathons to help you apply your knowledge.
How do I register for an event?
Go to the Events page, select the event, and click “Register Now” to book your seat.
Are recordings available after the event ends?
Recordings are available in your account after the event, where policy allows. You’ll be notified by email when they are ready.
Payment & Fees
What payment options are available?
We accept various payment methods including cards, transfers, and more. For details, check our payment policy page.
Are invoices and receipts provided?
Yes, you can download your official invoices and receipts from your dashboard once payment is completed.
Can I change my billing information?
Yes, billing info can be edited from your account’s settings page at any time before payment is processed.
Certificates & Verification
What are the requirements to get a certificate?
To receive your certificate, complete all course contents and pass the required examinations or projects.
Are certificates verifiable by companies?
Each certificate includes a code or link that can be used for direct online verification by employers or institutions.
Can I download or print my certificate as PDF?
Yes, certificates are available in PDF format for download and printing from your profile page.
Topics & Learning Paths
What learning paths are available?
  • Web Development (Front-end → Back-end → Full-stack)
  • Artificial Intelligence (Python → ML → DL)
  • Cyber Security (Networking Basics → Security → PenTest)
  • Networks (Basics → Administration → Enterprise)
Can I get guidance on the best path for me?
Yes, fill our short survey and get recommendations based on your background and goals. Personalized help is available.
Technical Support
How do I contact technical support?
Open a ticket through your account or email support@mzklearn.com for assistance. Our team is available 7 days a week.
I have a problem accessing a course, what do I do?
Try logging out and clearing your browser cache, then logging in again. If the problem persists, contact support with details.
Contact & Other Questions
How can I contact customer service?
Our email is support@mzklearn.com. You may also chat with our team via the contact page.
How do I change the language of the platform?
Change language settings from your profile. Multiple languages are supported including English and French.
